Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

WonderVerse Automation is intended for use by its owner to create and upload videos to YouTube.

Google account access

With the account owner's consent, the application requests the YouTube upload permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) to upload videos. It does not request access to passwords.

Credentials and data

OAuth credentials and tokens should be kept in private, access-controlled configuration, not in public pages or source code. The application sends video uploads and associated metadata to YouTube using Google's API. Google's privacy practices also apply to data processed by YouTube.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the application's access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the application's implementation changes. Any additional data processing should be described here before use.
